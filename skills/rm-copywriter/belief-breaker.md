# Belief-breaker script — myth / misconception / mistake (FRFRMU-1068, p30)

**Free content that makes people thirsty; sales copy tells them where to
buy.** For any reel drawing on a `false_beliefs` row (planner's `m_type`,
`step-03-10-false-beliefs-map.md` §7): name the M and the belief, evidence
the debunk, stop one step before the method, and end with the next step at
the right rung.

## 1. Name the M and the belief

The script's setup beat states which belief row it answers (cite
`item_id`) and which M it is — mistake, misconception, or myth.

## 2. Evidence the debunk — no evidence, no debunk

Every claim that a belief is false needs a citation: `source_ref: proof:<id>`
(`proof-and-claims.md`) or a dated web citation. **Domain claims — health,
finance, legal, or anything technical to the niche — get ONE ask-user
question** per FRFRMU-1051: *"Is this actually false in your field? What's
the evidence?"* Never assumed, never skipped silently.

## 3. The thirst test — belief shift, not the method

The reel delivers the BELIEF shift (free) and stops before the METHOD (the
product). Self-check + critic: does this reel teach the full how-to of what
the offer does? If yes, **re-cut one step earlier** and add the next-step
line — a belief-breaker that spills the method for free has a
`method_given: true` flag and cannot ship until re-cut.

## 4. Next step at the right rung

The CTA/next-step line points at the month's ONE conversion action
(FRFRMU-1047 §E) — bait for a cold audience, tripwire for warm — never the
backend rung, regardless of how ready the viewer feels after the debunk.

## 5. Demo variant

When the M is better SHOWN than told (a real product behaviour on camera),
the slot becomes a demo reel — no staged results, ever (Gate 1).

## 6. The enemy rule

A "mistake" is the OLD WAY's mistake — never a named person or account. The
`callout` taxonomy subcategory is a signal to double-check this rule wasn't
crossed (FRFRMU-1046's thin-niche enemy test applies here too).

## Validator (advisory)

- `check_thirsty_reel` — a belief-breaker script carries `m_type`, a belief
  row id, and an evidence ref; a regulated-domain debunk carries the
  constraints-screen note. **The `method_given: true` flag is hard** — a
  script flagged `method_given: true` cannot carry `status: shippable`
  until it is re-cut.

## Guardrails

- Evidence or no debunk. A wrong "myth" attacked without evidence is a
  credibility bomb — retract fast and visibly if evidence turns out wrong.
- Belief is free; method is the product. Say the thirst line, every time.
- Never punch at a person, only the old way.
- The niche's own data decides the M order in THIS niche; the planner's
  default is only a starting point.
