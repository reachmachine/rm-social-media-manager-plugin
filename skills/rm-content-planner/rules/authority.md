# Authority Rules — from Russell Brunson's *Expert Secrets*

> **What this is:** the universal, business-neutral principles from *Expert Secrets*, one section
> per book "secret" (Secret #1..#22), in our own plain words — a digest, not the book's text.
> Any content planner for any niche can cite these by number (e.g. "Secret #6").
> Each entry gives the principle plus one line on **how to use it in a content plan or a reel**.
>
> **Red line (compliance, applies to every rule below):** keep the *principle*, never a banned
> claim. Guaranteed-result and income/earnings promises are banned by ad platforms and get accounts
> shut down. Promise the *process and the dream*, never the *result*. Do not use "secret"/hype wording
> in the actual content — the numbered "Secret #N" labels here are only for citing this file.

---

## Secret #1: The Charismatic Leader / Attractive Character
You earn the right to lead by your results, not a title. Serve people who were one step behind where
you already are. Show where you came from and where you are now — flaws included; the gap is what moves
people. Take a clear, slightly polarizing stand, publish daily, and speak with certainty.
**In a content plan:** the account has a clear point of view and a recurring on-camera character
(the "Attractive Character") — origin, flaws, and stance shown, not hidden.

## Secret #2: The Cause
Point at the future your audience wants, not the stuck present. Give them a short rallying line and an
identity they can claim ("I'm a ___"). Name a common enemy. Let the message repel the wrong people so it
bonds the right ones.
**In a content plan:** a repeated cause/identity theme runs through the whole account, plus at least one
"here's what's possible" proof reel (the movement's first win).

## Secret #3: The New Opportunity
Sell a NEW way, not a "better" version of what people already tried and failed at. A better mousetrap is a
price war; a new vehicle is a fresh start. Every offer should raise the viewer's status, not make them feel
scolded for past failure.
**In a content plan:** frame the topic as a new path ("there's a different way to do this"), never as
"you're doing it wrong" — new-path reels convert; correction reels repel.

## Secret #4: The Opportunity Switch
Before selling, get one real result for one real person and capture the proof. Lock two things: the exact
RESULT people want and the VEHICLE that gets them there. Ask your market their #1 question and build around
what they *want*, not what you think they *need*.
**In a content plan:** answer the audience's most-asked question directly; lead with the result they crave
and the fear they want to avoid ("get X without Y").

## Secret #5: The Big Domino
Find the ONE belief that, once accepted, makes every other objection fall — the Big Domino. Ask a viewer to
believe only ONE thing; two beliefs halves your odds. Write it as a true if-then: *if they believe this one
thing, they have no option but to act.*
**In a content plan:** the whole account topples ONE core belief (example: a fitness coach's "you don't need
more willpower, you need the right system"). Audit every reel — does it reinforce that one domino, or add a
second thing to believe? Cut the second.

## Secret #6: The Epiphany Bridge
Lead people to the answer with a story, not a lecture — let them sell themselves. Build it around the moment
you first "got it." Sell on emotion first; logic justifies later. Strip jargon; tell it at roughly a
third-grade reading level and use "kinda like" to explain anything harder.
**In a content plan:** the origin/identity reel — your real "aha" moment — is the cornerstone; feelings and
scene come first, the point comes after.

## Secret #7: The Hero's Two Journeys
Every story names a Character, a Desire, and a Conflict. Show flaws early to build rapport. Tell two journeys
at once: the visible one (a goal everyone can see) and the hidden one (an identity change). Emotion comes from
conflict, not from the goal itself.
**In a content plan:** transformation reels carry both layers — the outward win AND the inner shift from
"old me" to "new me." Keep a reusable bank of these stories.

## Secret #8: The Epiphany Bridge Script
Tell each story in 8 beats, in order: Backstory, Desires, Wall, Epiphany, Plan, Conflict, Achievement,
Transformation. Name the outer struggle plainly, then the deeper inner one. Ask "why?" five or six times to
reach the real driver. End on who they became.
**In a content plan:** use this 8-beat skeleton for the founder/origin reel and any long story post — it is
the template, reused, tied back to the Big Domino.

## Secret #9: False Belief Patterns
List the exact false beliefs blocking your audience before writing anything. For each, trace the experience
that created it and the story they now tell themselves. Replace each with a short story where you once
believed the same, then found the truth. Keep an inventory of 10–20 of these.
**In a content plan:** objection-busting reels — one short story per false belief, mapped one-to-one so no
common objection is left unanswered.

## Secret #10: The 3 Secrets
Sort false beliefs into three buckets: **Vehicle** (does the method work?), **Internal** (can *I* do it?),
and **External** (forces outside me — time, money, luck). Pick the #1 belief in each and attach a story to it.
A demo is the best story for "I can't do it myself."
**In a content plan:** cover all three doubt types across the calendar — a "does this even work" reel, an
"I'm not good enough" reel, and a "no time / bad luck" reel — not just the obvious one.

## Secret #11: The Stack Slide
Design the irresistible offer FIRST. Brain-dump everything you could give, put a value on each item, and make
the total worth at least 10x the price. Value by RESULT, not cost. Add tangible tools (templates, checklists),
and make one item answer each doubt (Vehicle / Internal / External).
**In a content plan:** offer/CTA reels present a stacked, itemized value — what they get and what it's worth —
with a bonus that removes each objection, before any price appears.

## Secret #12: The Perfect Webinar
Build every pitch around ONE belief and hit it from many angles. Don't teach new ideas — only knock down the
false beliefs they already hold, in order (vehicle → self → outside). Open by building belief from the first
second, not at the close.
**In a content plan:** a series or sequence that repeats the one core belief in different framings and
dismantles doubts in order — origin first, then Vehicle, Internal, External.

## Secret #13: The One Thing
Script your opening word-for-word at first. Open on a curiosity headline ("how to [result] without [dread]").
Build fast rapport, state your inclusive goal up front, plant the Big Domino, qualify yourself briefly, tell
your origin story, then "liken it to them" (what's usually done, why it's hard, why your way is easy).
**In a content plan:** every reel's first 3 seconds carry a curiosity headline of that shape; the hook plants
the one belief before teaching anything.

## Secret #14: Breaking and Rebuilding Belief Patterns
In the teaching part, inspire and shift belief — don't dump the full how-to. Run the loop: State the point →
tell a quick story → show results → break the objection → restate the point as truth. Bust objections with
"You're probably thinking ___, right? Actually ___," 30–60 seconds each.
**In a content plan:** short reels that name a doubt out loud and flip it — aim for the shift from "I can't"
to "I can and I will," not just tactics.

## Secret #15: The Stack
When you present the offer, reveal items one at a time and re-show the growing stack each time, with a value on
each. Recap fast and high-level. Show three real case studies. Name and destroy the #1 reason people don't
start. Frame it as money SAVED, not spent. Build to 10x value before the price, then keep selling.
**In a content plan:** the closing/offer reel builds cumulative value, leads with proof (case studies), kills
the top blocker head-on, and frames the choice as what they save versus staying stuck.

## Secret #16: Trial Closes
Sprinkle tiny yes/no "trial closes" throughout — a small agreement roughly every 60 seconds. Make the FIRST
yes about the *problem* or the *dream*, never money. Reframe cost as a tool, time as the scarce thing, and the
choice as risk-free trial vs. doing nothing.
**In a content plan:** reels invite micro-agreement early ("sound familiar?", "you've felt this too, right?")
before any ask, and the ask itself is low-risk.

## Secret #17: The Perfect Webinar Model
Pick ONE core offer and run the SAME funnel repeatedly for a long stretch instead of a new angle every week.
Win the top of the funnel on CURIOSITY alone — intrigue without giving the answer away. Stack real
urgency/scarcity honestly. Forecast off who's still present at the pitch, and refine before you scale spend.
**In a content plan:** commit to one repeatable content engine and one promise; the top-of-funnel reel wins on
curiosity, not on explaining everything.

## Secret #18: The 4-Question Close (high-ticket only)
For high-priced offers, add an application plus a call rather than a one-click checkout. On the call: take
control, skip small talk, and ask four questions — their 1-year vision (drill "why" to the real driver), what's
held them back (disqualify people who only blame others), what resources they already have, then "Do you want
me to help you?" — and go quiet.
**In a content plan:** mostly out of scope for organic reels; use only if the account sells a high-ticket
offer — then content should pre-qualify and drive to an application, not a hard checkout.

## Secret #19: The Perfect Webinar Hack
You can build a whole selling presentation by answering 5 questions on a whiteboard: the new opportunity, the
Big Domino, a benefit headline with a concrete result and timeframe, an irresistible stack, and your origin
story plus three named belief-breakers. Rough and live beats polished and late.
**In a content plan:** a fast way to spec a content series — 5 answers on a page — then reuse the same
structure across reels, carousels, and email.

## Secret #20: Email Epiphany Funnels
Break your core stories and the offer into a sequence — origin story first, then one message per false belief,
then a stack-and-offer message. End every piece with a hook that pulls into the next. Structure matters more
than medium (text or video).
**In a content plan:** the same story order works as a reel series or a caption/DM sequence — cliffhanger
endings carry viewers from one post to the next.

## Secret #21: Epiphany Product Launch Funnels
For a launch, REUSE your proven stories — don't write new ones — poured into a set sequence: origin video, then
new opportunity, then internal beliefs, then external beliefs, then the stack-and-close. It's a delivery
wrapper, not a new argument.
**In a content plan:** a launch is a re-cut of your existing best stories into a fixed 4–5 part reel arc; keep
the persuasion identical, change only pacing and CTA.

## Secret #22: Fill Your Funnel
Don't build traffic from scratch — go where your audience already gathers. Build a "Dream 100" of list owners,
bloggers, podcasters, and influencers your people already follow. Warm them for months (follow, buy, give them
a platform) before any ask. Then promote via their audience, paid ads to their followers, or integration.
**In a content plan:** collaboration, guesting, and "spotlight" reels featuring the accounts your audience
already trusts — give value first, ask later; stay complementary, not competitive.

## Gate checklist (from this book)
A content plan is only "on-authority" when all of these are true:

1. **The account has an origin/identity reel** — a real Epiphany-Bridge story (Secret #6/#8), told feeling-first.
2. **The whole plan topples ONE core belief** — a stated Big Domino (Secret #5); no reel asks the viewer to
   believe a second thing.
3. **The offer is framed as a NEW way, not a "better" fix** — no correction/shaming reels (Secret #3).
4. **Objections are addressed as content, not ignored** — reels covering all three doubt types: Vehicle,
   Internal, External (Secret #9/#10).
5. **There is a recurring point of view and identity** — a clear stance and an "I'm a ___" theme people can
   claim (Secret #1/#2).
6. **At least one proof/transformation reel exists** — a visible win plus the inner shift behind it
   (Secret #2/#7).
7. **CTAs stack value and lower risk** — offer reels show what they get and what it's worth, and invite a
   small first "yes" that isn't about money (Secret #11/#15/#16).
8. **No banned claims anywhere** — no guaranteed results, no income promises; promise the process and the
   dream only (compliance red line).
